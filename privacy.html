Privacy Policy

RideTime

Effective August 21, 2026

Summary
Does RideTime collect personal data?

No. RideTime doesn't require an account, doesn't include any analytics, advertising, or tracking software, and doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data about you. This app's privacy nutrition label on the App Store reflects the same: no data collected.

What is stored, and where?

Your favorite parks and the "Hide Water Parks" setting are saved only in your device's local app storage (iOS UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device — it isn't sent to RideTime, ThemeParks.wiki, or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Where does the ride and wait-time data come from?

RideTime fetches live park and wait-time data from the public ThemeParks.wiki API. These are standard, unauthenticated network requests — RideTime doesn't attach any personal or device-identifying information to them beyond what any app's network request ordinarily includes at the network level (e.g. your IP address, visible to any server you connect to). ThemeParks.wiki is a separate, independently-run project; see their site for their own data practices.

Does RideTime use ads or third-party SDKs?

No. There are no ad networks, analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or other third-party tracking libraries integrated into RideTime.

Children's privacy

RideTime does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 — because it doesn't collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If RideTime's data practices ever change (for example, if a future feature requires collecting information), this page will be updated first and the "Effective" date above will change accordingly.
